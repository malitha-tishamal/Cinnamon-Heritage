Premium Essential Oils

Essential Oils

Pure Ceylon Cinnamon Essential Oils

Steam Distilled From Authentic Ceylon Cinnamon

Experience the natural power of authentic Ceylon Cinnamon through our premium essential oils, carefully extracted from cinnamon leaves and bark harvested from our family estate in Galle, Sri Lanka.

Cinnamon Leaf Oil

Pure Ceylon Cinnamon Leaf Oil

Our Cinnamon Leaf Oil is produced through steam distillation of carefully selected cinnamon leaves from our sustainable plantations. Rich in natural eugenol, this essential oil delivers a warm, spicy and clove-like aroma, making it ideal for fragrance, wellness, cosmetic and personal care applications.

Key Features
  • ✓ Steam distilled from authentic Ceylon cinnamon leaves
  • ✓ High natural eugenol content
  • ✓ Warm spicy aroma profile
  • ✓ Suitable for industrial and commercial applications
  • ✓ Available for bulk export supply
Main Constituents

Eugenol, Benzyl Benzoate, Linalool, Cinnamaldehyde (minor component)

Applications

Aromatherapy products, Natural fragrances, Cosmetic formulations, Personal care products, Wellness products, Home fragrance solutions

Cinnamon Bark Oil

Premium Ceylon Cinnamon Bark Oil

Cinnamon Bark Oil is one of the most valuable essential oils produced from authentic Ceylon cinnamon. Extracted from carefully selected inner cinnamon bark through steam distillation, it contains high levels of natural cinnamaldehyde, creating the rich, sweet and traditional cinnamon aroma recognized worldwide.

Key Features
  • ✓ Produced from premium Ceylon cinnamon bark
  • ✓ High cinnamaldehyde content
  • ✓ Strong sweet cinnamon fragrance
  • ✓ Premium ingredient for international markets
  • ✓ Suitable for food, fragrance and wellness industries
Main Constituents

Cinnamaldehyde, Eugenol, Benzyl Benzoate, Linalool

Applications

Premium fragrances, Food flavouring solutions, Beverage concepts, Wellness products, Natural aroma formulations, Cosmetic industries

Difference Between Cinnamon Leaf Oil & Cinnamon Bark Oil

Understanding the Two Essential Oils

Characteristic Cinnamon Leaf Oil Cinnamon Bark Oil
Source Cinnamon Leaves Inner Cinnamon Bark
Extraction Steam Distillation Steam Distillation
Main Component Eugenol Cinnamaldehyde
Aroma Warm, spicy, clove-like Sweet, rich cinnamon
Strength Mild to medium Strong and premium
Main Uses Cosmetics, fragrance, wellness Food, luxury fragrance, premium products
