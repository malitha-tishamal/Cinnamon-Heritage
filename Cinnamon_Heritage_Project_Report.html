CINNAMON HERITAGE

Industrial E-Commerce & Brand Ecosystem Report

Project Overview

Cinnamon Heritage is a sophisticated digital platform designed for a heritage cinnamon producer in Sri Lanka. It serves as both a luxury brand showcase and a high-performance e-commerce engine.

The system was built to solve the gap between traditional agricultural production and modern digital retail, providing customers with a transparent view of the "Farm-to-Table" journey while giving administrators granular control over operations, inventory, and analytics.

"Experience the true taste of heritage, powered by modern engineering."

Development Summary

The Cinnamon Heritage project stands as a benchmark for local industrial digitalization. By utilizing a high-efficiency Node.js backend and a bespoke frontend, the platform ensures maximum performance even with high traffic. The integration of administrative tools empowers the business to stay agile, responding to stock levels and customer inquiries in real-time.
